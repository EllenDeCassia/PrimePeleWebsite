COMMON QUESTIONS

Find answers to some of the most common questions about our mobile waxing services.

Where does Prime Pele provide its services?

Prime Pele provides mobile waxing services at clients' homes in Cape Town and selected surrounding areas.

How can I book an appointment?

Clients can contact Prime Pele through the contact form or WhatsApp to request an appointment.

Do I need to travel to a salon?

No. Prime Pele is a mobile service, meaning the service is brought directly to your home.

What waxing services are available?

Services include full leg, half leg, underarm, arm and bikini waxing. Please visit our Services page for more information.

How can I contact Prime Pele?

You can contact Prime Pele through WhatsApp, the contact form or the available social media channels.

Where can I find the service prices?

Service prices are available on the Services page. Please contact Prime Pele if you need additional information.
