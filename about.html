ABOUT PRIME PELE

Professional mobile waxing designed around your comfort, convenience and confidence.

OUR STORY

Prime Pele is a mobile beauty business based in Cape Town, providing professional waxing services directly at clients' homes. The business focuses on convenience, professionalism and customer comfort.

MISSION & VISION

Our Mission

Prime Pele's mission is to provide safe, professional and convenient mobile waxing services that enhance clients' confidence while delivering exceptional customer care in the comfort of their homes.

Our Vision

Prime Pele's vision is to become a trusted and recognised mobile waxing and beauty service in Cape Town and surrounding areas by providing convenient, reliable and professional services.
